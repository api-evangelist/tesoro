# Tesoro

Tesoro Corporation (later rebranded as Andeavor) was a Fortune 100 independent petroleum refiner and marketer headquartered in San Antonio, Texas. Founded in 1968, the company operated ten refineries in the Western United States with combined crude oil capacity of approximately 1.2 million barrels per day and approximately 3,000 branded retail gas stations. Tesoro rebranded as Andeavor in 2017 following its acquisition of Western Refining, and was acquired by Marathon Petroleum on October 1, 2018.

**Status:** Acquired (by Marathon Petroleum, 2018)

## APIs

No public developer API was offered by Tesoro Corporation or Andeavor. The company operated as a petroleum refiner and retail fuel marketer, not a software or platform company.
